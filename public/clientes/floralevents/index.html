FAQ

Good questions.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and large events, we recommend 6 to 12 months. For subscriptions or shop orders, a few days is usually enough.

Can I request specific flowers?

Yes. Every design starts with your preferences. We source your favorite flowers and build around your colors and theme.

What if I need to make changes?

At your reveal appointment you preview your florals exactly as they will look on the day. If anything feels off, we make changes, and we offer a second reveal at no additional cost.

Do you deliver outside Houston?

Tell us your venue and date and we'll confirm availability and delivery details for your event.
