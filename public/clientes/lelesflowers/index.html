About us

A little shop with a big heart.

Since she was a little girl, Selena Hull dreamed of owning a flower shop. Today, with her family working by her side every day, she gets the privilege of making someone's day, every day.

We lighten the darkest and saddest days, and the brightest ones too, with the gift of joy in flower form. This isn't just a job, it's a lifestyle. We take pride in what we do and always try our best to make every order top tier.

Thank you for supporting our small business. We love you all!
