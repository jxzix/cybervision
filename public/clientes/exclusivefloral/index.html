About us

A neighborhood florist, rooted in Houston.

Flowers have always been Corina's joy, arranging blooms for friends and neighborhood gatherings before she opened the shop. She brings that same care to every bouquet and arrangement.

Exclusive Floral Creations LLC focuses on quality floral design, careful sourcing and personalized service. Every arrangement is created with attention to flower selection, seasonality, color, style and vase choice, whether you have a specific vision or need help choosing the perfect one.

FAQ

Good questions.

What types of flowers and arrangements do you offer?

Everything from joyful celebrations and special milestones to heartfelt sympathy flowers and simple everyday gestures, in styles from elegant and understated to vibrant and cheerful.

Can I request a custom arrangement?

Yes. Whether you have a specific vision or want something completely custom, our team takes the time to listen and create something that feels personal and meaningful.

What if I need help choosing an arrangement?

Call us at (346) 763-3544 or visit the studio. We'll help you choose the right flowers for the occasion.

How fast can my flowers be delivered?

Many of our designs are available for next-day delivery. Call us and we'll confirm timing for your date and address.
