Meet our founder

Nino Shbeeb

Nino was raised with a deep appreciation for flowers, shaped by his family's roots in horticulture and his lifelong commitment to faith, craftsmanship and service.

After moving to Houston, Nino founded Flowers by Nino in 1998, where he began sharing his refined floral designs. With over 40 years of experience, his work is known for its timeless beauty, premium blooms and meticulous detail. His designs reflect gratitude, faith and a calling to serve others through florals.

FAQ

Good questions.

Do you offer same-day flower delivery?

Yes. We offer same-day delivery across Houston and surrounding areas in Harris County. Orders must meet the $100 minimum and are subject to a $30 delivery fee.

Can I request a custom arrangement?

Absolutely. Choose our Designer's Choice option at any price point, or request specific flowers, colors or styles. Our florists will design a custom piece that fits your vision and budget.

Do you offer arrangements under $100?

Yes. Arrangements for less than $100 are available for in-store pickup. Call 281-873-6466 and we'll have yours ready.

Do you deliver for weddings and events?

Yes. We design and deliver for weddings, corporate events and special occasions. Our team can also travel outside of Houston for larger events and destination weddings.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes. Flowers by Nino gift cards are the perfect way to let someone choose their favorite blooms. They can be purchased in-store, online or by phone.

Do you keep pre-made arrangements in-store?

No. Every arrangement is made fresh to order, ensuring the highest quality, freshness and attention to detail in every piece.
