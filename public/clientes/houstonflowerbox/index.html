How it works

What is a Designer's Choice?

Your florist hand-selects the color scheme, the flower varieties, the style and the container based on what is freshest this season.

Tell us the palette you love, from warm tones to pastels or all white, and let the designer do the rest. Because flowers are seasonal, substitutions of equal or greater value are sometimes needed, always keeping the look and feel of the arrangement.

Sympathy & funeral

Here when it matters most

Arrangements for services, homes and hospitals. A few details help us get it right.

Funeral services

  • Name of the deceased
  • Funeral home name
  • Date and time of the viewing

Funeral orders usually need extra lead time. Call us if you need a specific delivery time.

Hospital deliveries

  • Patient name and room number
  • Make sure the patient is not being released

We are experienced with hospital deliveries, though many ICU patients cannot receive flowers. Check with the hospital first.
