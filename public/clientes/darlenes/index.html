Our story

A Houston Heights family, still arranging.

Darlene's opened in 1969, and since 1982 it has been owned and run by sisters Susan Tate and Judy Bankhead, who were born and raised in the Houston Heights. Every arrangement is still designed by hand, to your colors, your budget and your occasion.

Whether you want a simple bouquet for a friend or the florals for an entire celebration, you are talking to the people who will actually make it.

  • Inspirational gifts
